# Backpropagation


As we are aware, the training of neural networks are to figure out a truth value, and discover how far are we from this truth value, and how should we then change our approach to the input data to get closer to the truth value. This is called [[gradient Descent]], where we nudge parameters of the neural network, to reduce loss (how far are we to the ideal answer)
