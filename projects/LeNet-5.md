# Convolutional Neural Networks

CNN's or Convolutional Neural Networks vary from traditional Neural Networks such as the Multi-layer Perceptron (MLP), as they bring in new form of invariance when swallowing in data, which is a Relational Inductive Bias.

Inductive biases help introduce solutions with properties we believe our model should have in order to generalize to future data. It's like when you see certain letter patterns in words, you automatically apply phonetic rules to know how they should be pronounced.
