# Common Math Notation

## Raw notes
- Lecture: #L01

Common mathematical notation used in the course:
- **Vector**: Denoted as $\vec{X}$
- **Matrix**: Denoted as $\underline{X}$
- **Scalar**: Denoted as $\text{X}$
- **Feature Vector**: A column vector representing a single row in a dataset:
  $$
  \vec{X} = \begin{bmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \\ \vdots \\ x_m \end{bmatrix}
  $$
