## Multilayer Networks

-   [[Multilayer Perceptron]] (MLP).
    -   Referred to as:
        -   Feed-forward neural network.
        -   Fully connected neural network.

-   Multilayer Neural Networks
    -   Capable of solving **XOR problems**, unlike single-layer perceptrons.
        -   By using hidden layers and non-linear activation functions
        -   The solution caused the MLP (Multilayer Perceptron) to be hard to train
        -   Solution : Backpropagation
