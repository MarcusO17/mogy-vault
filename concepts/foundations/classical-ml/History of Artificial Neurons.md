## History of Artificial Neurons
-   1943 - McCulloch & Pitt's neuron model
    ![](images/paste-4.png)
    -   Could solve AND, OR or NOT Problems
	$$

        \sum_{i=1}^{w}{x_iw_i} = Z

        $$

---

-   1957 - Frank Rosenblatt's Perceptron
    ![](images/paste-5.png)
    $$

    f_\theta\left(\sum_{i=1}^{m}x_iw_i\right) = \hat{y}

    $$
---

-   1960 - Widrow and Hoff's ADALINE
    ![](images/paste-6.png)
    -   A "single layer neural network"
       ![](images/paste-8.png)


-   1969 - Minksy and Pappert: "Perceptrons" Book
    -   Problem: Perceptrons (and ADALINEs) cannot solve XOR problems
        -   could only solve for binary boundaries.
        -   This lead to the first AI Winter, as Neurons seems to be a dead end.
