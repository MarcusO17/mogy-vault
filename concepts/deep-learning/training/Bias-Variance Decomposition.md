# Bias-Variance Decomposition

The definition of Bias-Variance Decomposition:

$$
\text{Bias}_{\theta}[\hat{\theta}] = E[\hat{\theta}] - \theta
$$
Let $\theta$ represent $y$. $\hat{\theta}$ is the model's prediction (e.g., $\hat{y} = \hat{f}(x; \mathcal{D})$), and the expectation $E[\hat{\theta}]$ is taken over all possible training datasets $\mathcal{D}$. This definition aligns with the bias term in the decomposition.
